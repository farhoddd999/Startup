Hearth test version

Thank you for testing Hearth.

Hearth helps parents practise tonight’s bedtime story in private, then read it to their child. This is an early test. Your honest reactions, especially when something is hard or confusing, decide what we build next.

Versión de prueba de Hearth

Gracias por probar Hearth.

Hearth ayuda a los padres a practicar el cuento de esta noche en privado, y luego leérselo a su hijo. Esta es una prueba temprana. Sus reacciones sinceras, sobre todo cuando algo es difícil o confuso, deciden lo que haremos después.

Open the appAbrir la appHow to send feedbackCómo enviar comentarios

Open Hearth on your phone

  1. Scan the code with your phone’s camera, or type the address under it.
  2. Allow the microphone if you want Hearth to follow your voice. You can also tap words instead.
  3. Put it on your home screen. Android (Chrome): tap the menu ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Voice works best in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone, in a quiet room. If Hearth can’t hear you, open Settings and choose I’ll tap words I need.

Abra Hearth en su teléfono

  1. Escanee el código con la cámara de su teléfono, o escriba la dirección que aparece abajo.
  2. Permita el micrófono si quiere que Hearth siga su voz. También puede tocar las palabras.
  3. Póngalo en su pantalla de inicio. Android (Chrome): toque el menú ⋮ y luego Agregar a la pantalla principal o Instalar app. iPhone (Safari): toque Compartir y luego Agregar a inicio.

La voz funciona mejor en Chrome en Android o Safari en iPhone, en un lugar tranquilo. Si Hearth no le escucha, abra Ajustes y elija Tocaré las palabras que necesito.

What to try

About 20 minutes in all. Tick things off as you go; this list is saved only on this device.

Qué probar

Unos 20 minutos en total. Marque cada paso al terminarlo; esta lista se guarda solo en este aparato.

Extra, if you have time: switch to Spanish in Settings, or record yourself reading a story for a night you work late.

Extra, si tiene tiempo: cambie a inglés o español en Ajustes, o grábese leyendo un cuento para una noche en que trabaje tarde.

Your feedback

Tell us what you think.

  1. In Hearth, open Settings and tap Send feedback. After story time you can also tap Tell us how it went.
  2. Tap a face, choose a few words, or record a voice message. You don’t have to write anything.
  3. You see exactly what will be sent. Send it to the person who invited you, or email us at hello@hearth.example.

There are no wrong answers. If something is confusing, that’s our mistake, not yours.

Sus comentarios

Díganos qué piensa.

  1. En Hearth, abra Ajustes y toque Enviar comentarios. Después de la hora del cuento también puede tocar Cuéntenos cómo le fue.
  2. Toque una cara, elija unas palabras o grabe un mensaje de voz. No tiene que escribir nada.
  3. Usted ve exactamente lo que se enviará. Envíelo a la persona que le invitó, o escríbanos a hello@hearth.example.

No hay respuestas incorrectas. Si algo es confuso, el error es nuestro, no suyo.

Your privacy in this test

Su privacidad en esta prueba

It stays on your phone

Your names, your progress and your recordings are saved only on your phone. We can’t see them.

Se queda en su teléfono

Sus nombres, su progreso y sus grabaciones se guardan solo en su teléfono. Nosotros no podemos verlos.

Your voice

In this test version, your browser’s speech service listens so Hearth can follow along. It may send the sound of your voice to the company that makes your browser (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari). Tap mode doesn’t use it.

Su voz

En esta versión de prueba, el servicio de voz de su navegador escucha para que Hearth siga la lectura. Puede enviar el sonido de su voz a la empresa que hace su navegador (Google para Chrome, Apple para Safari). El modo de tocar no lo usa.

Feedback is your choice

Hearth sends nothing by itself. Feedback goes only where you send it, and you see it first.

Los comentarios son su decisión

Hearth no envía nada por sí solo. Los comentarios van solo a donde usted los envíe, y usted los ve primero.

Start over any time

In Settings, Start over deletes everything Hearth saved on this phone.

Empiece de nuevo cuando quiera

En Ajustes, Empezar de nuevo borra todo lo que Hearth guardó en este teléfono.

For program staff and researchers

Running a test session

A 30-minute session with one parent at a time. Print this section and bring it with you.

Who to invite first

Start with staff and parents who are comfortable giving honest feedback. Before testing with families who find reading hard, put your program’s consent process in place. Invite everyone in a group the same way, so no one is singled out because of reading.

Before the session

  • A charged phone, or the parent’s own phone
  • A quiet corner, away from other parents
  • The QR card above, printed
  • A thank-you for the parent’s time
  • Your consent form, read aloud

Say this before you start

“We’re testing a new app that helps parents practise bedtime stories. We’re testing the app, not you. There are no wrong answers, and you can stop at any time. What you practise stays on your phone. If you want to share feedback, you choose what to send. Is it okay if I watch and take a few notes?”
MinutesWhat happensWhat to watch for
0–5Welcome, consent, open Hearth from the QR codeCan they open and install it without help?
5–10Setup: child’s name, look, short reading checkDo they use “press and hold to hear”? Do they skip words comfortably?
10–20Practise tonight’s story aloneWhere they pause, which hint helps (chunks, sounds or the word), anything that feels like a test
20–25Read the story aloud, to their child or to youConfidence compared with practice; do they use the question on each page?
25–30Talk, then send feedback together from SettingsTheir words, not yours

While you watch

  • Let them work through a hard word before you help; that’s what Hearth is for.
  • Don’t read words for them unless they ask.
  • Write down what they say, where they pause and what they tap.
  • If they seem embarrassed, switch to the demo reader in Settings and talk about it together instead.

Questions to ask at the end

  1. What was that like for you?
  2. When did Hearth help, and when did it get in the way?
  3. Would you use it tonight? What would stop you?
  4. Did anything feel like a test?
  5. What would you tell a friend about it?

Afterwards, send your notes with the parent’s permission, from Settings → Send feedback on your own phone, or by email to hello@hearth.example. Include the build number shown at the bottom of Settings.
