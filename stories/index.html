Story library

Stories worth reading twice.

Here are all 14 of our bedtime stories, from a first book with just a few words to two owls counting stars. Each one is written for a particular step, so a parent at that step can read every word. Type your child’s name below and they’ll be in the stories.

Names stay in your browser and aren’t sent anywhere. In the app, the pictures look like your family too.

How we make the stories

Written for one step, then checked word by word.

We wrote each story for one step. Then a computer check goes over every word, every time we change anything: each word has to be one you can sound out at that step, a heart word you’ve already learned, or a family name. The question on each page is there to get you and your child talking.

When the app is connected to our story writer, it can also write a brand-new story for one family. That story gets exactly the same check, and is rewritten until every word passes.
