What we don’t say

Numbers we decided not to use.

“54% of U.S. adults read below a sixth-grade level.” You’ll see this one a lot, but it mixes up two different scales (adult reading levels and school grades), and several news outlets have since taken it back.

“72% of children of low-literacy parents end up at the lowest reading levels.” It’s repeated everywhere, but we couldn’t trace it back to an original study we could check.

Any claim that Hearth works. Hearth is an early version and nobody is using it yet, so we can’t say how well it works. What we can point to is research on the methods it uses. Our first pilot is how we’ll find out about Hearth itself.

Where everything comes from.
