# Company setup checklist

General guidance, not legal advice. Rules vary by country and state; confirm each step with a lawyer and an accountant.

## Entity

- **Delaware public benefit corporation (PBC)**, with a stated public benefit such as: *"to help adults who struggle to read become confident readers through reading with their children."* A PBC lets the board weigh the mission alongside returns, works with standard venture financing, and signals to partners and families that the mission is protected.
- Alternative if the founder is outside the U.S.: a U.S. Delaware PBC as the parent company is still the usual choice for raising from U.S. investors.

## Founders and equity

- Founder stock purchase agreements with **4-year vesting and a 1-year cliff**, including for the founder.
- **IP assignment:** every founder, employee and contractor assigns inventions to the company. The code in this repository should be assigned at incorporation.
- Option pool sized for the first hires in the plan (typically 10–15% at pre-seed).

## Raising the pre-seed

- Standard instrument: post-money SAFE (valuation cap, optional discount), or a priced round if a lead prefers.
- Data room: this `docs/` folder, the deck (`pitch/`), the financial model, the prototype link.

## Before the first pilot

- [ ] Terms of service and privacy policy written for families (plain language, spoken versions) and for partners
- [ ] Data processing / data sharing agreement template (FERPA-aware for Head Start and schools)
- [ ] Research partner agreement and IRB approval for the evaluation
- [ ] Insurance: general liability, cyber, D&O once there is a board
- [ ] Open-source license review (CMU Pronouncing Dictionary: BSD-style; SUBTLEX-US frequencies; fonts under SIL Open Font License)
- [ ] Trademark search and filing for "Hearth" in education software classes; secure a domain and app-store names
- [ ] Accessibility review (WCAG 2.2 AA)

## Grants worth applying for

Literacy and early-childhood foundations, state adult-education innovation funds, federal SBIR (Institute of Education Sciences, NSF), and challenge prizes in adult literacy. Grants are modeled at $150K in Y1 in the financial model and are **not secured**.
