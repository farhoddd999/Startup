## The opportunity

Many of the families you serve include a parent who finds reading hard. They want to read to their children, and bedtime is when that difficulty shows. Hearth is a private reading coach on the parent's phone: the parent practises tonight's picture book alone for about five minutes with a coach that listens, then reads it aloud to their child. Every word in every story is one that parent can read.

We would like to run a free, 12-week pilot with [Partner name] to find out what Hearth does for your families.

## What families get

- A free app in English or Spanish that needs no reading to use: every button can be heard.
- Bedtime stories where their child is the hero, written for the parent's exact reading step.
- Help that waits: when they pause on a word, Hearth helps in small steps.
- Privacy: their practice stays on their phone. Nobody, including your staff, sees their mistakes.

## What your program gets

- A dashboard showing how many families read together each week, how their reading is growing, and who might welcome a check-in.
- A pilot report with outcomes for parents and children, usable for [PFCE / Measurable Skill Gains / your funder].
- Launch support: a family launch night kit, printed code cards, a 45-minute staff training, and a contact person at Hearth.

## What we ask of you

- Invite [30–60] families with a child aged 0–8; host one launch night (we bring materials and food).
- A family advocate who checks the dashboard weekly (about 30 minutes a week).
- Help with consent and the short baseline and week-12 measures, run by our research partner [name].

## Cost

Free for the pilot. After the pilot, if you choose to continue: $60 per enrolled family per year ($3,000 minimum per site), or as agreed.

## Privacy and data

Hearth shares only nights read, practice minutes, sounds learned and words-you-can-read counts, and only for families who agree. Recordings, mistakes and story text never leave the family's phone. We sign your data-sharing agreement and delete pilot data on request.
