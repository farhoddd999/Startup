# Go to market

## Positioning

**For** programs that serve young children and their families, **who** need parents to read at home but can't reach the parents who struggle to read themselves, **Hearth** is a private reading coach on the parent's phone, built around the bedtime story. **Unlike** adult-education classes or children's reading apps, Hearth helps the parent learn by doing what they already want to do every night: read to their child.

One line for families: **Be the one who reads the bedtime story.**

## Buyers and why they buy

| Buyer | Budget line | Why they buy | Champion | Typical size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head Start / Early Head Start grantees | Parent, Family and Community Engagement; training and technical assistance funds | Required family-engagement outcomes ("families as lifelong learners"), kindergarten readiness | Family services manager | 100–2,000 families |
| Public libraries | Early literacy, adult literacy, grants | Reach families who don't come to story time; adult literacy mandates | Youth services or adult literacy librarian | 50–1,000 |
| Adult education (WIOA Title II) | Program funds; state leadership funds | Daily practice and progress data between assessments; Measurable Skill Gains | Program director | 50–500 |
| Pediatric networks (Reach Out and Read clinics) | Partnership; health system community benefit | The book given at a visit becomes a book the parent can read | Clinic champion / medical director | referral |
| Medicaid health plans | Quality and community programs | Health literacy, family engagement, member experience | Community health / quality lead | 1,000–50,000 |
| State agencies | Early learning, adult education, literacy initiatives | Statewide family literacy at low cost per family | Agency literacy lead | 5,000–100,000 |

## Pricing (proposed; tested in pilots)

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Free for 12 weeks (2–3 sites) | Launch night kit, staff training, dashboard, evaluation report |
| Program | **$60 per enrolled family per year**, $3,000 minimum per site | Codes, dashboard, reports, family support line |
| Network / state / health plan | Volume pricing (target $40–55 per family) | All of the above plus data integration and outcome reporting |

Families never pay. Pricing anchors: Head Start's federal funding works out to roughly $17,000 per funded slot per year, so $60 is well under 1%; adult education programs spend far more per participant on instruction than Hearth costs per year.

## Motion

1. **Founder-led pilots (months 0–6).** Three anchor partners in one state, chosen for willingness to evaluate. Free in exchange for evaluation access and a reference.
2. **Land and expand (months 6–18).** Convert pilot partners to paid. Expand inside each network (other centers of the same grantee, other branches of the library system). Add Reach Out and Read clinics as referral partners.
3. **Policy and scale (18+ months).** Use pilot data for state contracts and health-plan programs; join state-approved vendor lists for early learning and adult education.

## Channels

- **Family launch nights** at partner sites: 30 minutes, food, a family advocate hands each parent a code card and helps them install Hearth. No email needed.
- **Staff training:** a 45-minute online session; a printed one-page guide; the dashboard's check-in list.
- **Conferences and associations:** National Head Start Association, Public Library Association, COABE (adult education), state early-learning networks.
- **Referral at the clinic:** a code card inside the Reach Out and Read book.
- **Word of mouth among parents:** a "share Hearth" card that never says why the parent uses it ("A bedtime story app our family loves").

## Pilot playbook

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| −4 | Partner agreement, data agreement, research consent materials |
| −2 | Staff training; print code cards and guides (English and Spanish) |
| 0 | Family launch nights; baseline measures |
| 1–12 | Weekly dashboard review with the family advocate; check-ins for quiet families |
| 6 | Mid-point survey (spoken, in-app) |
| 12 | Post measures; parent interviews; partner report |
| 13 | Waitlist group starts; conversion proposal to partner |

## 18-month targets

- 3 pilot sites → 15+ paying sites → ~3,000 families
- Week-8 active rate ≥ 50%; ≥ 2 nights read together per family per week
- 2 published case studies with outcome data
