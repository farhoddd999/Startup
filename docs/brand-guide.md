# Brand guide

## Name

**Hearth**: the warm centre of a home, where stories have always been told. Written in lowercase in the wordmark (**hearth**), title case in sentences (Hearth).

## Promise

**Be the one who reads the bedtime story.**

Supporting line: *Every night, two people get better at reading.*

## Mark

A flame rising from the spine of an open book: stories are where the warmth is. Files: `brand/hearth-mark.svg`, `brand/hearth-logo.svg`.

- Clear space: half the mark's height on every side.
- Minimum size: 20 px (mark), 96 px wide (logo).
- On night backgrounds use the full-colour mark with the wordmark in Cream; on paper backgrounds, wordmark in Ink.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `#141B30` | Bedtime screens, website night bands, deck covers |
| Night 2 | `#1C2542` | Secondary night surfaces |
| Paper | `#FBF2E4` / app `#FFF8EE` | The open book: daytime screens, reading surfaces |
| Ink | `#1E2A47` | Text on paper |
| Lamp | `#F4B860` | Accent on night; highlights; key calls to action on the website |
| Ember | `#E8833A` | Primary buttons in the app; the "read" underline |
| Ember deep | `#B0501C` | Eyebrows and accent text on paper (passes 4.5:1) |
| Gold | `#F2C14E` | Stars, sparkles, the flame |
| Sage | `#4F7F5B` | Quiet confirmations (never "correct/incorrect") |

Colour never carries meaning alone: a pill or label always says it in words.

## Type

| Role | Typeface | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Fraunces** (600) | Warm, soft serif with storybook character |
| Interface and body | **Atkinson Hyperlegible** | Designed by the Braille Institute for legibility; distinguishes similar letters |
| Story text | **Andika** | Designed by SIL for new readers: single-storey *a* and *g*, clear letter shapes |

## Voice

Warm, plain, adult. We speak to a proud parent, never down to them.

| We say | We never say |
|---|---|
| reader, parent, practise | illiterate, low-level, struggling learner, beginner |
| "Words to look at once more" | "Mistakes", "errors", "wrong" |
| "You're learning *sh*" | "You are at Level 3" |
| "Hearth helps when you pause" | "Hearth corrects you" |
| "Nights together" | "Streak" |
| "This is just for you" | "Your score" |

Rules: short sentences; active voice; name things by what parents recognise; no exclamation-mark cheerleading in the app (a little warmth, not hype); every instruction can be heard.

## Illustration

Flat, rounded shapes; soft palette; friendly animals; people who look like the family (five skin tones, six hair styles, five hair colours). Night scenes glow; nothing scary.

## Photography

None in the product. On the website and in decks, use the product's own illustrations and real screenshots. If photography is ever used, it shows real families with consent, never a parent looking ashamed.
