About Hearth

Every parent can be the one who reads the bedtime story.

Hearth exists to help adults who struggle to read become confident readers, by reading with their children. We start in the United States, in English and Spanish, and we’re building for the 739 million adults worldwide who lack basic literacy.

The name

Why “Hearth”.

The hearth is the warm centre of a home, where stories have always been told and passed down. Our mark is a small flame rising from the spine of an open book: stories are where the warmth is.

What we believe

Our commitments.

Dignity first

No levels, scores or red marks. We design for proud adults, never down to them.

Private by default

Practice stays on the phone. No ads, no data sales, ever.

Evidence over hype

We cite our sources, measure outcomes, and publish what we find, including results that disappoint.

Families never pay

Programs that serve families fund Hearth, so cost is never the reason a parent goes without.

Hearth plans to incorporate as a public benefit corporation, so the mission is written into the company’s charter.

Where we are

A working prototype. Pilots next.

Today

Built and working

The phone app, a 40-step phonics engine, 14 decodable stories, the story writer, a partner dashboard and the decodability checker on this site.

Next 6 months

Pilots and the phone app

Three pilot sites with about 150 families. An Android app with speech recognition on the phone. First Spanish stories.

Months 7–18

Evidence and scale

Published pilot results, an iOS app, and paid partnerships reaching about 3,000 families.
