Head Start and Early Head Start

Family engagement and parent literacy are required outcomes, and many of your families include a parent who finds reading hard. Hearth gives those parents a private way to read to their children every night.

  • Evidence for “Families as Lifelong Learners” and family engagement outcomes
  • Nights read together and parent reading growth, per family and for your program
  • Launch at a parent night; family advocates see who might welcome a check-in

Public libraries

Story time reaches families who already come in. Hearth reaches the ones who don’t, and supports your adult literacy and early literacy goals at the same time.

  • A code card at the desk, in the children’s area, or inside a take-home book
  • Pairs well with adult literacy tutoring and English classes
  • Reports for library boards and funders with families reached and nights read

Adult education programs

Your learners practise between classes with material they care about: their own child’s bedtime story. You see progress between formal assessments.

  • Daily practice that fits around work and childcare
  • Continuous progress data to target support before the post-test
  • A strong motivator for parents who want to read with their children

Pediatric clinics

Clinics that give a book at well-child visits put a book in the hands of parents who may not be able to read it. A Hearth card in that book changes what happens at home.

  • A simple referral: a code card with the book
  • No clinical workflow changes
  • Aggregate reports on families reached

Medicaid and health plans

Low health literacy costs the U.S. between $106 billion and $238 billion a year. Hearth builds everyday reading confidence in a way members actually want to use.

  • A member benefit built around families with young children
  • De-identified, aggregate outcome reporting
  • Volume pricing for plan-wide programs

Questions from partners

What programs ask us.

How do we know which parents need Hearth?

You don’t have to. Offer Hearth to every family as a bedtime story app. Parents who want help with reading get it privately; nobody is singled out.

What data do we get, and what stays private?

With each family’s consent: nights read, practice minutes, sounds learned and words-you-can-read counts. Recordings, mistakes, hints and story text never leave the family’s phone.

Does Hearth replace our classes or home visits?

No. Hearth is daily practice at home. It works alongside classes and home visits, and gives your staff a reason to start a warm conversation about reading.

What languages does it support?

The interface speaks and reads in English and Spanish. Stories are in English today, with Spanish stories next.

How is the evaluation run?

With a research partner, using a waitlist comparison so every family gets Hearth. We measure parent reading growth, nights read together and child vocabulary. See the evaluation plan.
