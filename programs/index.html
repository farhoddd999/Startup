Who we work with

Made for the people who already help these families.

Head Start and Early Head Start

Family engagement and parent literacy are already part of what you’re asked to deliver, and some of your parents find reading hard. Hearth gives them a private way to read to their children every night, and gives you something to show for it.

  • Evidence for the “Families as Lifelong Learners” and family engagement outcomes
  • Nights read together and how parents’ reading is growing, for each family and for the whole program
  • Easy to start at a parent night. Family advocates can see who might like a friendly check-in

Public libraries

Story time reaches the families who already come through your doors. Hearth can reach the ones who don’t, and it supports your adult literacy and early literacy work at the same time.

  • A code card at the front desk, in the children’s area, or tucked inside a take-home book
  • Works well alongside adult literacy tutoring and English classes
  • Reports for your board and funders: families reached and nights read

Adult education programs

Your learners get to practise between classes with something they really care about: their own child’s bedtime story. And you get to see progress between the formal assessments.

  • A little practice every day, around work and childcare
  • Ongoing progress, so you can step in before the post-test, not after
  • A strong reason to keep going for parents who want to read with their kids

Pediatric clinics

Many clinics give a free book at well-child visits. Some of the parents taking that book home can’t read it. A Hearth card inside the book can change what happens when they get there.

  • A simple referral: a code card goes in with the book
  • No changes to how your clinic runs
  • Summary reports on how many families you’ve reached

Medicaid and health plans

Low health literacy costs the U.S. somewhere between $106 billion and $238 billion a year. Hearth builds everyday reading confidence in a way members actually enjoy using.

  • A member benefit for families with young children
  • Outcome reports that are summarised and can’t identify anyone
  • Volume pricing for programs across a whole plan
Partner dashboard

See how families are doing, without seeing anything private.

  • How many families read together each week
  • How many words parents can read, and how that’s growing
  • Which families might welcome a friendly check-in
  • One click to export it all for funders and reports

What you’ll never see: recordings, the words a parent found hard, the help they asked for, or anything a child said. Families also decide whether to share a summary with you at all.

Questions programs ask us

Good questions, honest answers.

How do we know which parents need Hearth?

You don’t need to. Offer it to every family as a bedtime story app. Parents who’d like help with their reading get it privately, and nobody is singled out.

What data do we get, and what stays private?

Only with each family’s permission: nights read, minutes of practice, sounds learned, and how many words they can read. Recordings, the words they found hard, the help they asked for and the stories themselves never leave the family’s phone.

Does Hearth replace our classes or home visits?

No. Hearth is a few minutes of practice at home each day. It sits alongside your classes and visits, and it often gives staff a natural, kind way to start talking about reading.

What languages does it support?

Every button and instruction works in English and Spanish, and can be read out loud. The stories are in English for now. Spanish stories are next.

How will you know if it works?

We’ll run the evaluation with a research partner. Families start in two waves, so everyone gets Hearth, and the later group gives us a fair comparison. We’ll look at how parents’ reading grows, how many nights families read together, and children’s vocabulary. Here’s the plan.
