Privacy

This is just for you.

Many of the parents who use Hearth have kept their reading private for years. We built Hearth so it stays that way. These are commitments, not settings.

Our promises

Six things we will always do.

Practice stays on your phone

Nobody sees which words you found hard: not your child, not your program, not Hearth staff.

Your voice stays on your phone

In the Hearth app, speech recognition runs on the device. Recordings you make for your child are stored only on your phone and deleted with one tap.

No ads. No data sales.

Ever. No tracking pixels or advertising networks, in the app or on this website.

Programs see a summary, only if you agree

Nights read, practice minutes, sounds learned and your words-you-can-read count. You can stop sharing at any time; the app keeps working.

Children are not users

We don’t collect information from children. Your child’s first name stays on your phone and is used only to put them in the story.

No names sent to AI

When Hearth writes a new story, it sends only your reading step and your child’s interests. Names are placeholders, filled in on your phone.

About this prototype

One honest note.

The prototype on this website runs in your web browser and uses the browser’s own speech recognition. In some browsers, that sends audio to the browser maker. The app tells you this in Settings, and you can use tap mode or the demo reader instead. The Hearth app used in pilots replaces it with recognition on the phone before any family uses it.

This website doesn’t use cookies or analytics. The demo app saves its progress only in your browser.

This page summarises our commitments. A full privacy policy and partner data agreements will be published before our first pilot. Questions: hello@hearth.example.
