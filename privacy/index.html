Privacy

Your reading is your business.

Many parents who use Hearth have kept their reading to themselves for years. We built Hearth so it stays that way. Here’s exactly what happens to your information, including the parts of this early test version that aren’t finished yet.

Our promises

Six things we’ll always do.

Your practice stays on your phone

Nobody sees which words you found hard. Not your child, not your program, not anyone at Hearth.

No ads, and we never sell data

Not now, not ever. There are no tracking pixels and no advertising networks, in the app or on this website.

Nothing is sent without you

Hearth doesn’t send anything about you on its own. Feedback only goes where you send it, and you see exactly what’s in it first.

Programs only see what you agree to

Later, if you join through a program, you’ll be able to share a short summary with them: nights read, minutes practised and sounds learned. You can stop at any time and the app keeps working. In this test version nothing is shared at all.

Children aren’t users

We don’t collect information from children. Your child’s first name stays on your phone and is only used to put them in the stories.

We never send names to AI

When Hearth writes a new story, it only sends your reading step and the things your child loves. The story comes back with blanks where the names go, and your phone fills them in.

About this test version

One thing we want to be upfront about.

Right now, Hearth runs in your web browser and uses the browser’s own speech service to follow your reading. In some browsers, that means the sound of your voice goes to the company that makes the browser (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari). Hearth tells you this before you turn the microphone on, and again in Settings. If you’d rather it didn’t, tap mode works without listening at all.

Before any family uses Hearth in a pilot, we’ll replace this with speech recognition that runs on the phone itself, so your voice never leaves it.

This website doesn’t use cookies or analytics. The app saves its progress only on your device, and “Start over” in Settings deletes all of it.

This page is a plain summary of our promises. We’ll publish a full privacy policy and our data agreements with programs before the first pilot starts. If you have any questions, write to us at hello@hearth.example.
