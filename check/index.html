Why it matters

Most writing isn’t made for new readers.

A parent who’s learning “sh” can read only about 30% of a typical note home from school. Ordinary children’s books aren’t much better. That’s why every Hearth story is checked word by word before it reaches a parent.

How the checker works

  • It looks up how each word is said in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. About 19,500 common words are included here.
  • 214 spelling rules match the letters to the sounds, and the simplest match wins.
  • Every letter sound belongs to one of the 40 steps. A word counts as readable once all its sounds have been taught, or if it’s a heart word the reader has already learned.
  • It all happens in your browser. Nothing you paste in is sent anywhere.
