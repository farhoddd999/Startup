The problemFor millions of parents, bedtime is where it shows.

28%of U.S. adults at or below the lowest literacy level (2023), up from 19% in 2017
77%of U.S. adults at the lowest literacy levels have children
~300Kmore words heard by kindergarten when a child is read one book a day
739Madults worldwide lack basic literacy; two-thirds are women

Why help misses themNo one helps the parent holding the book.

Adult education reached about 1.2M people in 2023 and asks parents to show up, find childcare and admit they can’t read. Children’s AI reading tutors listen to the child. The federal family literacy program was defunded in 2011.

The insightEvery reading app listens to the child. Hearth listens to the parent.

A child at bedtime is the strongest reason an adult has to learn to read, and it comes back every night.

The productPractise in private. Read with pride.

  • Tonight’s story stars their child, and every word is one this parent can sound out, plus the new sound they’re learning.
  • Five private minutes: Hearth follows their voice and gives the smallest hint that helps: chunks, then sounds with pictures, then the word.
  • Story time: bedtime mode, silent hints, a question per page for the child.
  • Dignity by design: no scores, no reading needed to use it, English and Spanish, works offline.

TechnologyEvery word is checked first.

A phonics engine maps each word to its sounds (CMU Pronouncing Dictionary) across a 40-step, 120-pattern sequence. Claude writes stories for one family at one parent’s step; unreadable words go back for revision. A learner model tracks each sound per parent; a speech tracker follows the reader.

Built today: working phone web app, 14 decodable stories, 19,530-word lexicon, Story Studio, partner dashboard, automated tests. No users yet.

Business modelFree for families. Programs pay.

$60 per enrolled family per year (proposed), paid by Head Start programs, libraries, adult education, pediatric networks and health plans. Estimated cost to serve ~$5 per family per year; target gross margin ~90%. A 300-family Head Start grantee is an $18,000 contract.

Buyers today: Head Start ($12.3B, 715,873 slots, FY2024), Reach Out and Read (4.6M children), adult education (1.2M learners).

  • Months 6–18: paid contracts, ~3,000 families, iOS, Spanish stories, published results.
