Evidence plan

We will measure two learners, not one.

The parent

Words they can read, from Hearth’s engine. Steps gained. Pre and post scores on the assessments partners already use.

The child

Nights read together and words heard. Expressive vocabulary, pre and post, with a research partner.

The family

How often they read together, and the parent’s confidence reading aloud, from short surveys in the app.

First pilot: 12 weeks, 3 sites, about 150 families, compared with a waitlist group. Results go to partners first, then out in public.

We’ll be judged on outcomes, so we’re designing for evidence from day one. Two learners, not one. For the parent: the engine already measures the words they can read and steps gained, and we’ll add pre and post scores on the assessments partners already use. For the child: nights read, words heard, and a standard expressive-vocabulary measure with a university partner. For the family: reading frequency and parent confidence. The first pilot is 12 weeks, three sites, about 150 families, with a waitlist comparison group.
