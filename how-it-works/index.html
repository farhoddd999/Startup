How it works

Practise in private. Read with pride.

Hearth turns tonight’s bedtime story into a short private lesson for the parent, then gets out of the way so the child hears their parent read. Here is every step, and a live demo you can tap.

Step by step

From the first tap to “Goodnight”.

1 · Getting started

A private start, no reading needed

Hearth talks the parent through setup in English or Spanish. Every button can be heard by pressing and holding it. The first screen after the welcome is a promise: this is just for you.

  • The child’s name, and what the child calls the parent
  • Pictures that look like the family: skin tone and hair for both
  • A short private check of up to 13 words finds the right first story. Skipping is fine, and no level is ever shown.
2 · Tonight’s story

Written for this parent, starring their child

Each night Hearth picks the story the parent is ready for: every word is one they can sound out with the sounds they’ve learned, a “heart word” they know by sight, or their family’s own name. Tonight’s new sound appears several times.

With a connection, Hearth can write a brand-new story for the family. The engine checks every word, and anything the parent can’t read yet goes back for a rewrite.

3 · Practise alone

A coach that listens, and never judges

The parent reads aloud. Hearth follows along word by word, ignores repeats and talking, and moves only forward. If the parent pauses, help comes in small steps: chunks after 3 seconds, sounds with pictures after about 6, and the word itself only after 10.

  • “Listen” reads the page aloud with each word lit up
  • Tap any word for help at any time
  • Heart words show a 💛 and are never sounded out wrongly
4 · Ready for tonight

A few words to look at once more

At the end, Hearth counts the words read on their own and with a hand, without a percentage, and shows up to four words to look at again. Tonight’s new sound comes with its picture and key word.

5 · Story time

Bedtime mode: your child hears only you

The screen dims and the text grows. Hints become quiet marks only the parent notices, with no sound unless they turn it on. After each page a short question turns the story into a conversation, which is what builds a young child’s vocabulary.

  • Turning pages early never counts against the parent
  • Record tonight’s reading for nights you work late; it stays on the phone
6 · Your sky

Progress without a single score

Each sound the parent can read is a star. One honest number grows over time: the words you can read out of the 5,000 most common in English. Nights together and words your child heard are counted too.

Under the hood

Four parts, all working today.

Write

Story writer

Claude writes a story for one family at one parent’s exact step. No names are sent: the child is a placeholder filled in on the phone.

Check

Phonics engine

Every word mapped to its sounds against a 40-step, 120-pattern sequence. Unreadable words never reach the parent.

Listen

Reading coach

Follows the reader’s voice, tolerates repeats and chatter, and gives the smallest hint that helps.

Learn

Learner model

Tracks each sound per parent. A sound counts as learned only after good reading on two different days.
